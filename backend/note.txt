Important: Modular Monolith doesn't mean we ignore scalability, security, performance, or reliability. We implement these inside our single backend application and its infrastructure.

Our goal is to build a production-ready monolith that can handle the project's expected traffic and can be scaled later without prematurely introducing microservices.
